# CropSim kernel: validation and model change report

Generated by `tools/validation/validate.py` on the Scottsbluff test parcels. Do not edit by hand; rerun the script.

## Summary

- **Validation.** 20 test parcels x 25 years (500 parcel-years per model) on real Scottsbluff weather, 2001-2025. With every fix on (the default), every check passed: no NaN or infinite values in any water budget output, the water balance closes every day and every year (kernel check and an independent check from the daily series and carried soil state), annual totals equal the daily and monthly sums, no negative fluxes, the second pass reproduces the first, and results are repeatable and thread safe.
- **Original v8.0 for contrast:** 292 of 500 parcel-years do not close the water balance by more than 0.005 in, mostly irrigated parcels (transpiration above the water taken up) and wet covers (ET with no source).
- **Plausibility.** 20 of 20 parcels fall inside the rough ranges for their crop and irrigation type. Flags and observations are in Part 1; none is a kernel defect, but some are worth a look before calibration.
- **Biggest movers** (largest change in a parcel's 24-year mean, fix on its own): `WETLAND_BALANCE` (wetland supply +42.12 in/yr on P18); `ROTATION_FREQUENCY` (gross irr. -30.62 in/yr on P11); `TAILWATER_FURROW` (deep perc. -8.17 in/yr on P11); `FORAGE_CUTOFF` (gross irr. -3.65 in/yr on P12); `TRANSPIRATION_LIMIT` (et -1.30 in/yr on P02).
- **No change on these parcels:** `DRAINAGE_SPREAD`, `INFILTRATION_OUTPUT`, `ET_ACCUMULATION`, `PERENNIAL_ROOTS`, `RZMIN_CAP`.

Inputs are plausible test data, not State parcel data with known answers: representative soils, Panhandle crop calendars and irrigation settings, and crop coefficient curves after Wright (1982). The numbers show how the kernel behaves and how much each fix moves results on realistic inputs; they are not calibrated estimates.

### Observations

- **Irrigation losses leave the soil budget.** Gross irrigation minus the water that soaks in (about 2.8 in/yr on pivots, 5-7 in/yr on furrow and 9-13 in/yr on border rotation parcels) is not runoff, deep percolation or ET in CropSim's balance; it simply leaves. The parcel layer (WP4) has to decide where that water goes (evaporation and drift, tailwater, or seepage to groundwater) before writing recharge.
- **Deep percolation under demand-scheduled irrigation is near zero** (0-0.5 in/yr for corn, 1.8 in/yr for potatoes on loamy sand), because demand scheduling refills only the depletion less a rain allowance and the 9 ft profile below the roots is rarely wetted to field capacity. Most irrigated recharge will come from the second pass, where metered applied water can exceed demand, and from the application losses above.
- **Dryland transpiration is low** (0.7-2.4 in/yr) and evaporation takes 85-95% of precipitation. ET equals precipitation on average, so recharge and the water balance are fine, but the split between evaporation and transpiration (and so yield) looks off for a 15 in climate. Soil evaporation draws the top 12 in down to air-dry. This is the same with or without the fixes; it is a calibration item (evaporation parameters, air-dry contents, residue).
- **Off-season evaporation counts.** CropSim runs the whole year, so annual ET includes 5-7 in/yr of evaporation outside May-September on irrigated parcels and about 10 in/yr on pasture. Compare growing-season ET (May-Sep column) with published crop ET.
- **Wet covers draw groundwater.** With the fixes, riparian woodland and cattail wetland ET (49 and 45 in/yr) is supplied mostly by WETSUPS (42 and 38 in/yr), with 4-6 in/yr of rain draining back. The groundwater model should use supply minus deep percolation.
- **Wheat after a fallow year coded as crop 15 loses its spring crop** with the fixes (P20). The original's FALLOW_CROP error set the 'live crop' flag after fallow, so the next wheat year started at green-up (15 March). Fixed, the flag stays off and the wheat year is treated as a fall planting: transpiration near 0 and yield 0. Coding wheat-fallow as winter wheat every year (P13) is unaffected. If the State's land use data codes fallow years separately, the parcel layer should set `state.livecrop = 1` before a wheat year that follows fallow (tested: this restores the green-up start).
- **BVALUE is infinite for crops 12 and up** (pasture, range, woodland, wetland), in both the original and the kernel: it divides by seasonal ET for yield (ETMAX), which is 0 for those crops. It is a yield-model output only, but the parcel layer should not store it as is. A small kernel fix (report 0) is an option.
- **Original v8.0, leap years:** the crop coefficient on 31 December is 0, outside KCL-KCU, because the original never simulates that day. LEAP_CALENDAR fixes it.

## Method and limits

- Model: the CropSim compute kernel (`fortran/kernel`) through `cropsim.Kernel`. Fix.ORIGINAL is bit-identical to the frozen v8.0 program (tests/test_parity.py).
- One kernel call per parcel per year, soil state carried from the previous year; rotations change the SIM each year and keep the state.
- The second-pass parcel (P05) takes P01's daily gross irrigation from the run with every fix on as known applied water, so its fix comparisons hold the applied water fixed, as metered data would.
- Means are over 2002-2025. Per-year changes for every fix, parcel and year are in `fix_impact_years.csv`.
- Plausibility ranges are a sanity screen, not calibration targets.
